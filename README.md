# Fikretak – Youth & Investors Platform

## Overview
**Fikretak** is a modern web-based platform that connects young entrepreneurs who have innovative project ideas with investors looking for promising opportunities.

The platform bridges the gap between ideas and investment by creating a space for collaboration, mentorship, and funding discovery.

---

## Problem Statement
Many young entrepreneurs face challenges such as:
- Lack of funding  
- Limited access to mentors  
- Difficulty reaching investors  

Meanwhile, investors struggle to find organized and trustworthy startup ideas.

---

## Solution
Fikretak solves this by:
- Showcasing startup ideas in a clear and professional way
- Allowing investors to browse and filter projects easily
- Connecting entrepreneurs with funding and mentorship opportunities

---

## Target Users
- **Entrepreneurs:** Young people with startup ideas who need funding or advice  
- **Investors:** Individuals or groups looking to invest in innovative projects  

---

## Key Features (MVP)
- User Authentication (Register / Login)
- Role Selection (Entrepreneur / Investor)
- Project Submission
- Project Browsing and Filtering
- Investor Interest System
- Basic User Dashboard

---
